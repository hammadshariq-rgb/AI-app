Summary: Callisto AI is a voice-powered desktop assistant. We collect only what is necessary to provide the service. Your conversation history is stored locally on your device. Voice recordings are processed for transcription and not permanently stored. We never sell your personal data.

1Who we are

Callisto AI is a product of Shariqen, operated by Hammad Shariq. We are based in Canada and Pakistan. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use the Callisto AI desktop application and associated services.

By using Callisto AI, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2What data we collect

We collect the following categories of personal information:

Data typeWhat we collectWhy
Account information Name, email address, profile picture (via Google Sign-In or email registration) To create and manage your account
Voice data Audio recordings captured when you activate the microphone To transcribe your speech into text for the AI to process
Chat history Your conversations with Callisto AI To provide context-aware responses and session history
Location data Approximate location (city/region level) when requested by features To answer location-relevant queries (weather, local info)
Google Contacts Contact names and phone numbers (only if you connect Google Contacts) To enable calling or messaging contacts by name
Payment information Billing details for subscription payments To process payments (handled entirely by Stripe — we never see raw card numbers)
Usage data Feature interactions, error logs, app version To improve the app and diagnose issues

3How we use your data

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and improve the Callisto AI service
  • Authenticate your identity and manage your account
  • Process your voice input and generate AI responses
  • Enable integrations you choose to connect (Google, Spotify, etc.)
  • Process subscription payments via Stripe
  • Send you important service-related notifications
  • Diagnose technical issues and ensure app stability

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the service.

4How we store and protect your data

  • Voice recordings are sent to our secure server for transcription processing only. They are not stored after transcription is complete.
  • Chat history is stored locally on your device. It is not uploaded to our servers unless you explicitly use a cloud sync feature.
  • Account data is stored on secure servers hosted by Railway (cloud infrastructure provider) with encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest.
  • Authentication tokens are stored using your operating system's secure credential storage (Windows Data Protection API).
  • Payment data is handled entirely by Stripe and never stored on our servers.

We implement industry-standard security practices including HTTPS for all data in transit and access controls on our backend systems.

5Third-party services

Callisto AI integrates with the following third-party services to deliver its features. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • OpenAI — Powers AI responses and voice transcription. openai.com/privacy
  • Google — Used for Sign-In with Google, Google Calendar, Google Drive, and Google Contacts (only if you connect them). policies.google.com/privacy
  • Stripe — Processes subscription payments. stripe.com/privacy
  • Spotify — Music playback integration (only if you connect it). spotify.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Railway — Cloud hosting for our backend server. railway.app/legal/privacy

We only request permissions from third-party services that are necessary for features you choose to use.

6Data sharing

We may share your data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers — Third-party services listed in Section 5, solely to provide the features of the app
  • Legal requirements — If required by applicable law, court order, or governmental authority
  • Business transfer — In the event of a merger or acquisition, with notice provided to users beforehand

7Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access — Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction — Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion — Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Portability — Request your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent — Disconnect any third-party integration at any time from within the app

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hammad.shariq@hotmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

If you are located in Canada, your rights are governed by the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

8Children's privacy

Callisto AI is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately and we will delete it.
