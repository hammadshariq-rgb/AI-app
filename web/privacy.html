3How we use your information

  • To answer your questions and carry out the actions you ask for
  • To generate images, 3D models and videos from your descriptions
  • To upload content to your connected accounts, only after you confirm
  • To sync your settings, memories and recent chats across your devices
  • To manage your account, subscription and plan limits
  • To send service messages, such as when something you created is ready
  • To diagnose problems and protect the Service against misuse

We do not sell your personal information, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.

4Google, YouTube and Google API Services

If you choose to connect a Google service, Callisto asks for only the permissions that feature needs:

  • Sign in with Google (openid, email, profile): to identify your account and confirm that every Google connector uses the same Google account.
  • YouTube, read-only (youtube.readonly): to show you your own channel statistics and recent videos when you ask.
  • YouTube upload (youtube.upload): to upload a video you chose to your own channel. Callisto shows a confirmation card with the video, title, description and visibility (Private by default) and uploads only when you press Publish. It does not edit, delete or manage your other videos.
  • Google Calendar (read-only) and Google Analytics (read-only): to show your events and site statistics when you ask.

Google access tokens are stored on your device and used only for the requests you make. They are not stored on our servers except briefly while a sign-in is completed.

Limited Use: Callisto AI's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data for advertising, do not sell it, do not let humans read it except with your permission or where required for security or by law, and do not use it to train AI models.

Callisto uses YouTube API Services. By using YouTube features in Callisto you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke Callisto's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions or by disconnecting in Callisto's Connectors screen, which deletes the stored tokens.

5Instagram and TikTok

  • Instagram (through Meta): to show your business account statistics and, when you confirm, publish a photo or Reel you chose to your Instagram business or creator account. Instagram requires media to be fetched from a web link, so the file is placed on our server at a private, random address for up to one hour and deleted as soon as Instagram has collected it.
  • TikTok: to show your account statistics and, when you confirm, upload a video you chose. Depending on TikTok's approval of our app, the video is either posted or sent to your TikTok drafts for you to finish.

We never post, comment or message on your behalf without your confirmation. Your use of these platforms is also covered by Meta's Privacy Policy and TikTok's Privacy Policy.

6Where your information is stored and for how long

  • On your device: tokens for connected accounts, full chat history and your "Artifacts" (copies of images, 3D models and videos you created, which are cleared every Monday). Deleting the app or signing out removes these.
  • On our servers (MongoDB, hosted on Railway): your account, subscription status, synced app data (Section 2) and usage counts. We keep this while your account is active and delete it within 30 days of an account deletion request, except where we must keep billing records by law.
  • Temporary files: photos sent for video generation and media being posted to Instagram are held in memory for at most one hour, then deleted.
  • Voice: we do not keep audio recordings. They are sent for transcription and discarded.
  • AI providers may keep request data for a limited time under their own policies, for example to detect abuse (see Section 7).

9Security

All connections use encryption (HTTPS/TLS). Sign-in tokens are protected with your operating system's secure storage where available. Server access is restricted, and third-party secrets are kept on the server, never in the app. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your devices and accounts protected.
