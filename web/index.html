WATCH THIS
Stuck on an error, a form, a spreadsheet? Say "watch this". Callisto reads your whole screen — including the small print, which is usually the bit that matters — and tells you what it would actually do: the button to press, the field that is empty, the line the error is on.

It then keeps an eye on it. While you work it checks the screen every few seconds on your own machine, and speaks up only when something has genuinely changed — so it helps when you move on, and stays quiet when you have not.

You can hand it a job at the same time — "watch my screen and tell me how to add this scope to Google Cloud" — and it answers that, about what it can see.

A bar sits at the bottom of the screen the entire time it is looking, showing how many times it has looked, with a Stop button. Say "stop watching" and it stops. It is told never to read password or payment fields, and it only ever looks because you asked it to — never on its own.

ASSIST MODE
Press Alt+Shift+C and Callisto keeps half an eye on your screen while you get on with your work. It does not talk, it does not read anything back to you, and most of the time it says nothing at all. When it does see something worth doing, a small card appears in the corner.

What it speaks up about. An arrangement in a message — "Diarise ‘Meeting with Sam’? Tomorrow at 5 pm". A wrong answer in homework — it checks the arithmetic itself before saying so. A misspelt word. A paragraph that reads like a machine wrote it. Something to summarise.

How you answer it. The card lasts three seconds. Press it and it happens; press the cross, or simply carry on working, and it does not — running out of time is a no. Nothing is ever done without you pressing it.

Callisto cannot type into another program's window, so a correction goes to your clipboard instead: press the card, then Ctrl+V where it belongs. Calendar entries and reminders go straight in.

It appears over other apps only, never over Callisto itself — if Callisto is in front of you, you can simply ask it. Press Alt+Shift+C again to stop, and it stops on its own after an hour.

QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

What is Callisto AI?

Callisto AI is a voice-first AI assistant for Windows and Mac. You talk to it and it answers out loud, remembers what matters to you, reads your business numbers, opens your apps and files, and makes pictures, posters, videos and 3D models.

Does Callisto work on Windows?

Yes. Callisto runs on Windows 10 and Windows 11, and on macOS. There is also a web version with fewer features, because some things need access to your computer.

Is Callisto AI free?

The app is free to download. The free plan includes 15 messages a day and 2 images, 2 videos and 2 3D models a week. Premium costs $20 a month or $200 a year.

What can I do by voice?

Ask questions, open apps and files, set reminders and tasks, check your calendar, get your daily update, see your numbers, draw, make images and videos, edit a video, and post to your social accounts. Everything can also be typed.

Which accounts can Callisto connect to?

Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Google Analytics, Stripe, Shopify, Squarespace, Google Calendar and Spotify, among others. Nothing is posted until you press Publish.

Can Callisto help with marketing and my business?

Yes. It can write hooks, scripts and pitches, work out profit margins, size a market, and critique your posts. Say "I'm back" in the app for a daily update with a marketing note based on your own numbers.

Does Callisto remember me?

Yes. It picks up lasting facts as you talk, like the name of your business. The memory orb in your account shows everything it remembers, and you can add or remove anything.

Is my data safe?

Passwords are hashed, two-factor sign-in is available, and connected-account tokens are encrypted. Video editing runs on your own computer, and card details are handled by Stripe. The privacy policy lists what is collected and who processes it.
